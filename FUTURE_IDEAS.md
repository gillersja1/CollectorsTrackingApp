# Future Ideas & Roadmap

Notes on potential features to explore next for the Collectors Tracking App:

1. **Barcode & QR Code Generator**
   - Generate printable QR codes or barcodes for collectibles inside the app.
   - Useful for labeling physical items, shelves, or display cases.

2. **Multiple Photos per Collectible**
   - Expand image handling from a single photo to a full photo gallery per item (e.g., front, back, box, certificate of authenticity).

3. **Condition & Grading Tracking**
   - Add condition grading selectors (e.g., *Mint*, *Near Mint*, *Good*, *Poor*).
   - Support grading company scores (e.g., *PSA 10*, *CGC 9.6*, *BGS 9.5*).
